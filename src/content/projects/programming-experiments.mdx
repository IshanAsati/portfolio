## Overview

Alongside research and application work, I experiment with Python and Pygame. These are the projects that exist mainly because a question was fun to pursue: a small game loop, a physics sketch, an interaction that does not need a business case.

The experiments are deliberately small. The goal is to finish enough of an idea to learn from it, not to inflate a prototype into a product.

## What belongs here

The section will collect:

- Small game experiments built with Pygame.
- Interactive programming sketches.
- Programming exercises that produced something visually or technically interesting.
- Prototypes made to understand a mechanic, a pattern or a tool.

It will not collect tutorials copied from elsewhere, unfinished code presented as a finished project, or fabricated screenshots. If something is a work in progress, it will be labelled as one.

## Planned directions

These are directions I want to explore, not published projects:

- **A small arcade game** with a complete loop: start, play, lose, retry.
- **A sprite and animation study** built around a single character.
- **A physics or particle sketch** focused on feel rather than realism.
- **An interactive toy** with no score, no win state and no particular purpose beyond curiosity.
