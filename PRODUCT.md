# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and internship screeners evaluating Ishan Asati. They arrive from a CV, GitHub, a referral or a competition, usually with limited time, and need to understand the scope, stage and credibility of the work before deciding whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

An editorial record of Ishan's projects and experiments across AI, software engineering, hardware and creative programming. It exists to turn a time-boxed scan into a reason to reach out: a visitor should leave understanding what was built, how it works and how finished it really is, then have an obvious route to email or GitHub.

## Positioning

A field journal rather than a résumé or project gallery. The differentiator is documented honesty: every project appears at its real stage, and results travel with their limits — a research study is not a medical device, a mocked pipeline is named as mocked, and an empty notes shelf stays empty. Breadth across AI, software and hardware is tied together by one method: build to understand.

## Operating Context

Visitors move from a chaptered homepage narrative into individual case studies, then to the work index, about page or contact page. The site is a static web publication with no backend and no contact form; email and GitHub are the direct channels. Case studies are long-form and include screenshots, diagrams and technical detail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site built from MDX content and deployed on Vercel; no server, accounts or form handling.
- Seven project entries: DermoAI (research study), Atlas Fit and Atlas (in development), StudyFlow and Halcyon (complete builds), Robotics & Electronics (prototypes and concepts), Programming Experiments (reserved placeholder).
- DermoAI is not clinically validated. Its historical results are 82.79% reported accuracy and 87.30% cancer-class sensitivity and must not be reframed as clinical evidence.
- The Notes collection is intentionally empty until real writing exists; it must not be filled with placeholder articles.
- No invented projects, metrics, testimonials, screenshots, credentials or deployment claims.
- Case-study copy describes each project at its real stage, including mocked or unfinished parts.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Ishan Asati; the site speaks in the first person, plainly and without hype. "I build to understand" and "document honestly" are established commitments: the work is shown with its limits rather than polished into false completeness. GitHub is github.com/IshanAsati.

## Evidence on Hand

- Seven MDX case studies under `src/content/projects/`, with real project screenshots and diagrams under `src/assets/`.
- A portrait of Ishan under `src/assets/`.
- Public repositories on GitHub, plus live demos for Atlas (account-gated hosted build, public landing page) and Atlas Fit.
- DermoAI's first-place result in the DAV ML Khanna machine learning competition.
- No testimonials, client logos, press coverage, formal credentials or notes exist on the site; future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before adjectives: show the build, the result and the limit.
2. Respect the screener's time: scope, stage and proof must be legible within a fast scan.
3. Never promote a study, prototype or placeholder into a finished product.
4. Every chapter earns the next step: the narrative should end with a clear reason and route to reach out.
5. Let the work carry the page; the interface should recede behind the projects.
